Elli's House

How to Play — PC

Move: A / D or ← / → · Jump: Space · Crouch, slide and read signs: S or ↓ · Dash: W + direction · Pause: Esc

How to Play — Mobile

Use the virtual D-pad to move, crouch and read signs · JUMP jumps · DASH + direction dashes · PAUSE pauses

Escape the house in this platform adventure originally made by the Glacial Moon Games team for GameJaaj 7.
