About me

Hi, i am Daniel Giovanni, the game designer responsible for the Glacial Moon Games, i am deeply in love with gaming in general, video games, board games and all that surround them, always trying to show my love for the media in my projects.

I am now releasing my biggest project yet, Insectorama, an arcade-style game for Android. I spent years developing this game, creating its art and code by hand, with the help of great artists and programmers along the way.
